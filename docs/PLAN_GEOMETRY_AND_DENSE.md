## A. Separate reconstruction errors from export/display errors (run first; no pipeline change until A passes)
A1 Export unit tests, `tests/test_export_geometry.py`, fabricated reconstruction with non-trivial poses (rotation 37 deg about a skew axis, |t| ~ 3, three cameras, five points):
- `T_world_camera[:3,3] == -R^T t` and `T_world_camera[:3,:3] == R^T` to 1e-9; `inv(T_world_camera)` reproduces `cam_from_world`; a point projected with `cam_from_world` lands on its stored 2D observation to 1e-6 px (axis and order check, x right / y down / z forward).
- Same transform for points and cameras: apply one random Sim(3) to the fabricated reconstruction and export again; distances camera-to-point must scale by exactly s for every pair (no separate handling of the two sets).
- Time order: fabricated image names and ids in conflicting orders (id 1 = latest timestamp). Exported `poses[k].order` must follow `timestamp_s`, never `image_id` or name; `first_seen` in the PLY must follow the same order. Today `export.py` sorts by `im.name`, correct only because names equal selection order; the test pins the contract.
- Intrinsics after resize: `scale_camera` with calibration 1920x1080 and frames 960x540 gives fx, fy, cx, cy exactly halved and distortion unchanged; `trajectory.json.camera.width/height` equals the JPEG size on disk for a real session (`mq_sumida_0025_0125`).
- PASS: all tests pass; FAIL: any mismatch is a bug in export/display and is fixed before B-D. Evidence: `outputs/evidence/geom/pytest-export.log`.
A2 Viewer (`apps/desktop/static/viewer.js`): framing radius is max(p90 point distance, 1.1 x max camera distance), so one outlying camera (the FSC 59.7-70.4 s stretch) shrinks the whole scene. Change: default "main observed region" = max(p90 point distance, p95 camera distance); toggle "full extent" = max over all points and cameras; near plane relative to the chosen radius. Caption shows `raw N / filtered M / drawn D` (from the PLY count, the filtered PLY of C5, and `lastDrawn`) plus the frame mode. Point size and zoom are labelled "display only" and are never written to any report or claimed as geometry change. UI smoke: both modes render the FSC session, counts text present, drawn <= filtered <= raw.
A3 Decision rule: a complaint reproduced on the fabricated data (A1) or changed by framing only (A2) is an export/display defect; one that survives both is a reconstruction defect and goes to B.

## B. Per-segment diagnosis (`python -m mapping.diagnose <session> --window 5 --step 2.5`, read-only on existing files; output `diagnosis_segments.json` + `diagnosis.png`)
Sliding windows of 5 s stepped 2.5 s over the declared segment; per window on the largest model: (1) triangulation angle distribution of points whose track touches the window (`calculate_triangulation_angle`; p10/p50/p90 in degrees); (2) track length p50 and share of 2-view points; (3) reprojection p50/p95; (4) share of points with positive depth in every observing camera (expect 1.0; < 0.99 is a flag); (5) focal and k1 of the shared camera (one value per run; also from a per-window refit with `bundle_adjustment` on that window's images only, to see whether focal drifts); (6) feature distribution: share of keypoints in the upper 25 % of the image (sky), in detector person boxes (`packages/vision`), and in the bottom 12 % band (subtitles/logos), plus a low-texture share (keypoints in cells with < 5 features); (7) depth consistency: median depth of points first triangulated inside the window vs median depth of points tracked in from before it (ratio `new_over_old`); (8) image-flow vs reconstructed-motion consistency, defined without ground truth: for each consecutive registered pair, rotation-compensated image flow `phi = median |x' - warp(x; K R_rel K^-1)| / f_px` (rad, measured on the source frames at the pair's timestamps), reconstructed angular motion `psi = |C_{i+1}-C_i| / d_i` with `d_i` the median depth of the co-visible points; `r_i = psi / phi` is dimensionless and should be constant inside one consistent model. Window flag `LOW_CONFIDENCE` when median r is outside [1/3, 3] x the model median, when `new_over_old` is outside [1/3, 3], when positive-depth share < 0.99, or when angle p50 < 1.0 deg. Frozen expectation for FSC: windows covering 59.7-70.4 s carry at least one flag, and windows before 55 s carry none; if neither holds, the drift inference is recorded as not confirmed and the jumps stay unresolved. Sumida: zero flagged windows (otherwise the detector is too strict and is reported, not tuned silently). Segment labels are written to `coverage.json` (`low_confidence_windows`) and drawn on the timeline; nothing is bridged, smoothed, clamped or straightened.

## D. Dense route on CPU (poses from GOOD sessions or GOOD segments only; FSC flagged windows excluded)
D0 `patch_match_stereo` probe: run once on a 3-frame subset, store the exact exception text and pycolmap version in `outputs/evidence/dense/patch_match_probe.txt`; never retried on this machine.
D1 Pair selection: consecutive or skip-1 registered pairs inside unflagged windows with baseline / median co-visible depth in [0.05, 0.30] and >= 100 shared tracks; list in `dense/pairs.json`.
D2 Rectified stereo per pair: undistort with the refined shared camera (k1), `cv2.stereoRectify` from the SfM relative pose, SGBM (block 5, 128 disparities at 960 px width, uniqueness 10, left-right check <= 1 px); depth in model units = f_rect x B / disparity.
D3 Verification: (i) against sparse points of the pair: `|d_sgbm - d_sfm| / d_sfm <= 0.10` for >= 70 % of them, else pair REJECTED; (ii) third view: warp the depth into the next registered frame and compare with that frame's own SGBM depth, keep only pixels with relative difference <= 0.10; a pair keeping < 30 % of its valid pixels is REJECTED. Record per pair: valid %, sparse-agreement %, third-view-kept %.
D4 Fusion of kept pixels only, voxel size = median nearest-neighbour spacing of the sparse points; `dense_verified.ply` labelled `DENSE_VERIFIED`; unverified SGBM output stays in `depth_preview/` labelled `DEPTH_PREVIEW_UNVERIFIED` and is shown in its own panel. Meshing (`poisson_meshing` or Delaunay on the fused points) only on `dense_verified.ply`, trimmed to vertices within 2 voxels of a fused point; unobserved areas remain holes; label `MESH_FROM_VERIFIED_DENSE`. Four labels (sparse / depth preview / verified dense / mesh) are never mixed in one file.
D5 Optional monocular depth (Depth Anything V2 Small, local CPU/MPS, no paid API): per frame, fit scale+shift of inverse depth to the frame's sparse points; accepted as `ALIGNED_PREVIEW` only if >= 50 sparse points, median residual <= 10 %, and warping into the next registered frame agrees within 10 % on >= 60 % of overlapping pixels; otherwise it stays `DEPTH_PREVIEW_UNVERIFIED` and is never fused. Scale remains RELATIVE even when aligned.
Acceptance (Sumida): >= 20 pairs selected, >= 50 % of pairs pass D3, `dense_verified.ply` has >= 10 x the sparse count of the covered frames, and its points re-project within 1 px into their source pair on a 500-point sample. Below that: FAIL recorded, sparse map unaffected. FSC: dense only over unflagged windows; the 59.7-70.4 s stretch produces no dense output by rule.

## E. Reporting contract
Every experiment row records before/after on the same inputs: registered ratio, points3D (raw and filtered), mean reprojection, time-normalised jumps, flagged windows, MAP seconds, and for D the pair counts above. Improvement = the numeric criteria in C or D met with the Sumida guard intact. Unresolved = any FSC flag that remains after C1-C4, reported as "reconstruction and image motion disagree in 59.7-70.4 s; cause not confirmed" with the B numbers attached. Nothing is merged across models, no trajectory is post-processed, and the quality thresholds of `map_report@2` stay unchanged; a new schema version is required if any threshold changes. Log: `docs/VALIDATION_LOG.md` rows plus `outputs/evidence/geom/` and `outputs/evidence/dense/` raw files.
