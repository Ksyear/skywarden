# Research survey: densifying a static map from fixed poses and sky/person masks (2026-11-09)

Condensed from `outputs/evidence/static_map/research_notes.md`, a read-only web and local survey written before the S2 run of `docs/PLAN_STATIC_MAP.md`. Marks are kept as written there: **VERIFIED** = read in the source, documentation or checked locally; **UNVERIFIED** = not confirmed. The column "tried here" is filled from `outputs/evidence/static_map/summary.json` and the plan's Result section; a method that was not run says so.

Problem the survey addressed (plan section 1, findings 1–2): the Skywarden CPU plane sweep (ZNCC 7x7, fronto + ground hypotheses, best 2 of 4 neighbours, multi-view depth consistency, voxel fusion) with sky/person keep-masks lost structure next to the sky because a masked pixel anywhere in the 7x7 window dropped the pixel, the near floor stayed thin, and a border artefact produced a floor streak. Camera poses come from a frozen pycolmap 4.2.1 SfM model (SIMPLE_RADIAL, 1600x900, about 150 frames per 60 s clip) and are never re-estimated.

## What the measurements say about the survey's ranking

The survey ranked the changes smallest first: (1) masked ZNCC, (2) bilateral weights, (3) per-pixel source weighting, (4) COLMAP-style filter and fusion, (5) ACMP planar priors, (6) OpenMVS as a benchmark, (7) DA3-BASE/SMALL as a gated prior.

- (1) was run and is the core of S3r40. It recovers most of the sky-edge band that B2 lost (B2 0.28–0.66 → S3r40 0.63–0.88 against B1 0.74–0.95) and, with border exclusion and all-sparse neighbour choice, removes the floor streak. It does not reach B1 on structure, wall or band, and the valid-pixel cost alone costs 1.6x matching time. The remaining band loss is now attributed to the segmentation (bare branches labelled sky, about 4 px edge at 384 px), not to the matching rule; S3r's brightness-based restoration of dark pixels inside the sky label brought the band from 0.60 / 0.69 to 0.63 / 0.72 on the segments (S2n → S3r), and lowering the valid-window share to 0.40 to 0.67 / 0.75 (S3r40).
- (6) was run as a separate AGPL command-line process. Its floor is better and cleaner than every Skywarden variant on the segment (0.385 vs 0.252, no floor point behind the fitted plane, `float_fs` neutral 0.235 vs 0.374), its structure is almost absent (0.118 vs 0.689), its default resolution crashes on this machine, and on the full clip its near floor disappears (0.018 on f000240). A union with S3r40 gains floor where OpenMVS has it (+11 pp on two frames) at 303 s and is not integrated.
- (2), (3), (4), (5), (7) were not run. (3) is the plan's S5 and was dropped for time; (7) is blocked by missing torch and the xformers dependency on this Mac and limited to the Apache-2.0 SMALL/BASE checkpoints if ever run; (4) and (5) have no run and no plan row.
